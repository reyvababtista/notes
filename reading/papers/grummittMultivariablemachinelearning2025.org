#+begin_quote
An important advantage is the balance between bias and variance compared to traditional predictive models like logistic regression or single decision trees.
#+end_quote

#+begin_quote
Bias refers to errors introduced when a model is too simplistic, missing key patterns in the data. Variance, on the other hand, reflects errors from a model being overly sensitive to small fluctuations in the training data, leading to predictions that do not generalise well.
#+end_quote
